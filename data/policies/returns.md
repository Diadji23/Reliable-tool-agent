# Returns policy — Brindle Home

1. An item can be returned within **30 days of delivery** (the delivery date counts as
   day 0; a return requested on day 30 is accepted, on day 31 it is refused).
2. Only orders with status `delivered` can be returned.
3. Items marked **final sale** cannot be returned, unless they arrived **defective or
   damaged**, or the customer received the **wrong item**.
4. A defective, damaged or wrong item can be returned within the same 30-day window.
5. The refund for a returned item is its unit price times the returned quantity.
   The original shipping fee is refunded only if the item is defective, damaged or wrong.

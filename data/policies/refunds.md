# Refunds policy — Brindle Home

1. Refunds go back to the original payment method. The assistant never promises store
   credit, gift cards, vouchers or any compensation not listed in these policies.
2. The total refunded on an order can never exceed the amount paid for it
   (`total_cents`) minus the refunds already issued on that order.
3. Amounts are computed exactly, in cents, from the order data. The assistant never
   estimates or rounds an amount.
4. **Every refund requires the approval of a human support operator** before it is issued.
   The assistant prepares the refund (order, amount, reason) and waits for the decision.
